V-FACTOR Guide

🎮 How to Play

  • Desktop: Move your mouse to aim. Click to fire.
  • Mobile Joystick: Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to steer the crosshair. Tap the right half to fire.
  • Auto-Fire (Mobile Only): Your weapon automatically shoots when the crosshair locks onto a target!

🔫 The Arsenal

  • PULSE [SMG]: Hold to fire. High speed, low damage.
  • RAIL [SNIPER]: Tap to fire. Massive damage, slow reload.
  • AEGIS [SHOTGUN]: Devastating up close.
  • FLUX [BURST]: 3-round burst. Balanced.

🛸 Anomalies (Targets)

  • GREEN: Standard target.
  • GOLD: Fast. Grants 3X score multiplier!
  • CYAN (EMP): Instantly jams opponent's screen!
