V-FACTOR Guide

🔫 The Arsenal

  • PULSE [SMG]: Hold to fire. High speed, low damage. Best for combos.
  • RAIL [SNIPER]: Tap to fire. Massive damage, screen shake, slow reload.
  • AEGIS [SHOTGUN]: 6-pellet spread. Devastating up close.
  • FLUX [BURST]: 3-round energy burst. Balanced range and power.

🛸 Anomalies (Targets)

  • GREEN: Standard target.
  • GOLD: Moves fast. Grants perfect hit audio & 3X score multiplier!
  • CYAN (EMP): Instantly jams opponent's screen!

🎮 How to Play with Friends

  • 1. Host a 1v1 DUEL game.
  • 2. Copy your ID and send it to your friend.
  • 3. Friend pastes the ID and clicks Connect.
  • 4. Friend clicks READY (Hold) in the Match Room.
  • 5. Host sets the Match Time and clicks START!
